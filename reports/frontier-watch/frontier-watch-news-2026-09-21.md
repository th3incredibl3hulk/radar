## Anthropic discloses Claude now leads 26% of its own R&D — a recursive-improvement number, not a demo

`anthropic` `agents` `strategy` `capability-jump` `signal` · **Source:** [Bloomberg: Anthropic Says Claude Drives 26% of Its Research and Development](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-17/anthropic-says-claude-drives-26-of-its-research-and-development) · *Found: 2026-09-17*

Anthropic published internal metrics on Sept 17 showing Claude now "leads" 26% of the company's AI research and engineering work — meaning it completes most of a given task end-to-end from a high-level prompt under human supervision — up from under 1% in February. Claude collaborates with staff on roughly 90% of all R&D work; ~30,000 agents are doing research/engineering work at Anthropic at any given time, with every action passing an online monitor before execution and 100% reviewed after the fact by an offline monitor. Of over one billion agent decisions logged in August, roughly 1 in 47,000 (0.002%) was blocked.

**So what:** This is the first time a frontier lab has put a hard, tracked percentage on "AI building the next version of itself" rather than a one-off anecdote — and the trajectory (under 1% to 26% in seven months) is the more important number than the snapshot. If Anthropic's own R&D velocity is increasingly gated by how fast it can supervise autonomous agents rather than by human researcher headcount, that has direct implications for how fast the frontier can move overall, and it's worth asking your own AI vendors for an equivalent number rather than assuming this stays Anthropic-specific.

## JPMorgan upgrades Meta after Muse Spark cracks the App Store top 3

`meta` `strategy` `agents` `signal` · **Source:** [Yahoo Finance: JPMorgan Changed Its Mind on Meta Stock After This Major AI Breakthrough](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/jpmorgan-changed-mind-meta-stock-140002556.html) · *Found: 2026-09-19*

JPMorgan upgraded Meta from Neutral to Overweight on Sept 19 and raised its price target from $640 to $820, directly citing the success of Muse, Meta's personal AI agent built on the Muse Spark model line (launched Sept 8, previously covered as Muse Spark 1.3). Muse reached #3 in the U.S. App Store shortly after launch; JPMorgan's note argues Muse Spark performs meaningfully better than the models Meta had been shipping and can now compete directly with OpenAI's and Anthropic's offerings.

**So what:** This is the first external, market-priced validation of Meta's strategic bet — abandoning the open-weight Llama line for a closed, proprietary Muse family after Llama 4's benchmark-gaming controversy — and it's coming from an analyst upgrade, not a benchmark leaderboard. If a major bank is now willing to underwrite "Meta caught up" as an investment thesis, treat Meta as a live top-tier contender again for any evaluation you'd previously excluded it from by default.
